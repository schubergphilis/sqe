**STATUS 2026-10-09 (cl): hash joins that outgrow query memory retry as sort-merge joins (`feat/adaptive-join-fallback-784`, #784 phase 1).** DataFusion 55.1's hash-join build cannot spill, and inexact Iceberg estimates miss build sizes by 3x to 150x, so a large build under `query.max_query_memory` ended in ResourcesExhausted. Now, when a read query fails that way before its first batch and its plan holds an equi hash join that is not null-aware, `join_fallback::FallbackStream` drops the attempt. It plans the same policy-enforced logical plan again with `prefer_hash_join = false`, on the coordinator, under a fresh quota, and streams that instead. Both the buffered and streaming paths are covered, and the finalizer reports the retry's plan. The fallback is on by default; `query.join_fallback = false` turns it off, and it stays off without spill files. Two additions to the design: each sort's unspillable merge reservation is sized to a quarter of the quota over the plan's sorters (1 MB floor, configured ceiling), and the retry narrows `target_partitions` (never below 2) until every sorter gets 4 MB, because many sorters under a small cap fail with "Not enough memory to continue external sort". A 1 GB cap with ten sorts per partition keeps 25 partitions. Metrics: `sqe_join_fallback_total{outcome}` and `sqe_join_fallback_wasted_seconds_total`; each retry logs a warning. `tests/it/join_fallback_e2e.rs` joins two 1.5M-row Hive CSV tables under a 16 MB cap: it fails without the fallback and returns the exact answer with it, on both paths. **Not yet:** an audit-entry field and an EXPLAIN ANALYZE note for the retry; SF10 q09 at a 1 GB cap; distributing the retry (phase 2).
