**STATUS 2026-09-16 (at): what an agent can rely on through `execute_sql_readonly`, measured and written down (`docs/mcp-agent-functions`).** The sidecar's list, checked against a release build over Glue-backed Parquet: `sha256(lower(col))` (SQE's own function, HMAC under `[policy] mask_key`, plain SHA-256 without it), `digest(col, 'sha256')` (Binary), both on `Utf8` and `Utf8View`; `INTERSECT` of two `DISTINCT` hash sets works and needs `IS NOT NULL` on both sides because `sha256(NULL)` is `NULL` and `INTERSECT` matches `NULL` with `NULL` (verified: one spurious overlap without it); `COUNT(DISTINCT)` and `approx_distinct` both work on Boolean, Int32/64, every Decimal128 precision present, Date32, Timestamp(µs), Utf8, Utf8View, List and Struct, agreeing within 1 percent; `get_table_stats` is `SHOW STATS FOR` and reports Glue's `numRows`/`totalSize` when a crawler or `ANALYZE` left them, the planner's listed size when Trino wrote `-1`. Item 4 of the same list, the correlated scalar subquery on a CTE alias that failed with `No field named s.<col>`, planned and ran in eleven variants on this build (DISTINCT, GROUP BY, ORDER BY LIMIT, a join in the CTE, two subqueries, `s.*` over struct/list columns); the one shape that fails is a non-equality correlation on a second outer column (`Physical plan does not support logical expression ScalarSubquery`), a DataFusion decorrelation limit, documented with the rewrite.
