**STATUS 2026-10-09 (ct): the join-fallback retry shares its quota fairly (`fix/join-fallback-fair-share-784`, #784).** `join_fallback_e2e` flaked in CI with "Not enough memory to continue external sort". The per-query budget is greedy, so in the sort-merge retry one sorter could take the whole spillable share (13.1 MB of 13.4 MB in CI), or one side's spill merge could take the rest. The other side's sorter was then refused while it held nothing, so it had nothing to spill and failed. Which side starved depended on scheduling. The retry's budget is now built `HeadroomBudget::with_fair_share()`: each consumer that adapts to a refusal (a spillable one, or an `ExternalSorterMerge[n]`, which merges fewer files per pass) is capped at the limit over the registered consumers once past its first reservation. Consumers that fail when refused stay greedy, and normal queries keep the greedy budget. New deterministic tests in `memory_headroom` and `query_memory` fail without the share.
