**STATUS 2026-10-09 (cj): EXPLAIN classifies its inner statement (`fix/explain-carries-inner-kind`, #811).** `EXPLAIN` and `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` were classified as `Utility` without looking at the inner statement. `EXPLAIN FULL` carried its inner as text, also unclassified. The coordinator's `explain::require_query` re-parsed the inner text later and refused anything but a query. Now the classifier does it: the `Explain` arm classifies the inner AST through `classify`, and the `EXPLAIN FULL` arm parses and classifies its inner text. Either form of a non-query is refused with `NotSupported` and one message. It was `ExecutionFailed` before, so Trino now reports `NOT_SUPPORTED` and Flight `Unimplemented`, as for a write hidden in a query. `require_query` is gone. `every_door_reaches_the_same_verdict` (front_door) runs plain, CTE, EXPLAIN / ANALYZE / FULL, write-in-query and multi-statement shapes through all four wire dialects and requires one verdict. The doors already agreed before the change, so the change makes the refusal happen during classification, before any handler runs. The Trino handler's stream-or-buffer routing in `sqe-server` used the un-normalized `parse_and_classify_typed`, so a Trino-only SELECT shape took the buffered path. It now goes through the front door. Open: an `EXPLAIN FULL` inner is classified as written, without the Trino folds, so a Trino-only inner shape (`TABLE t`) is a syntax error, as it already was in the handler. **NEXT:** #784 (bound large hash builds), per #814.
