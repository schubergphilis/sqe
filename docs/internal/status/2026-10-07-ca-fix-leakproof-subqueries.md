**STATUS 2026-10-07 (ca): EXISTS and IN (subquery) no longer switch the row-filter guard on (`fix/leakproof-subqueries`, #805).** Both only answer true/false/NULL for the outer row, so `leakproof.rs` now counts them as leakproof; `IN (subquery)` still counts as fallible when the outer and subquery types do not meet in a lossless widening. Scalar subqueries stay fallible ("more than one row" depends on the data). Subquery bodies are still walked as plans of their own (`apply_with_subqueries`), so a fallible predicate inside one keeps the guard on (`fallible_predicate_inside_a_subquery_is_guarded`). **Guard cost, TPC-H SF1 A/B** (`tests/it/guard_ab.rs`, `#[ignore]`, local Parquet, plain plan vs a trivially-true row filter on `lineitem`, median of 11 interleaved runs): only q08, q12 and q14 trip the guard (most TPC-H arithmetic sits in aggregate arguments, which run above the filter), and they land at 0.95/0.99/1.00x plain; the suite total is 0.99x, inside run-to-run noise. Pruning loss on a partitioned Iceberg table is not covered by this rig.
